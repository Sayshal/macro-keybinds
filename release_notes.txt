## Bug Fixes

- Assigning a key already used by another macro now unbinds that macro right away and keeps it unbound after reload, including when the new macro has not been saved yet.
- Browser-protected keys (F5, F11, F12, PrintScreen, ScrollLock, NumLock, CapsLock) are rejected with Foundry's protected-key warning.
- Control, Shift and Alt pressed with a key are now recorded, and keybind labels use Foundry's key names with modifiers in a fixed Control, Shift, Alt order (`Shift+;` rather than `Shift+:`).
- Pressing Delete or Backspace in the keybind field now unbinds the macro right away, and a macro bound again later uses its new key after reload instead of a stale one.
